Vince Carter

Vinsanity's Dunk Contest

Vince Carter became famous for his explosive athleticism and creative dunks. His performance at the 2000 NBA Slam Dunk Contest is widely remembered as one of the event's most exciting displays.

  • Won the 2000 NBA Slam Dunk Contest
  • Represented the Toronto Raptors in the contest
  • Known for his powerful one-handed and between-the-legs dunks
