Michael Jordan

Air Jordan and the Slam Dunk Contest

Michael Jordan is one of basketball's most celebrated players, known for his athletic dunks and competitive style. He won the NBA Slam Dunk Contest in 1987 and 1988.

  • 1987 and 1988 NBA Slam Dunk Contest champion
  • Played most of his career with the Chicago Bulls
  • His 1988 contest win came in Chicago, where he defeated Dominique Wilkins in the final
